Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a photoshoot or to ship samples?

Approved product photos can be enough to get started. Send clear front, back and detail shots, plus any styling references. For flowing dresses, abayas or layered outfits, a short phone clip showing the drape or movement can also help.

How do you keep the garments accurate and the styling modest?

Share your requirements for neckline, sleeves, opacity, coverage and outfit styling. We agree on those before production and review the draft against your product references. AI can vary fine details, so flag things like embroidery, logos and fastenings early.

Which package fits my launch?

Choose Signature Reel for one hero look, Collection Launch for up to three looks, Ad Creative Pack for testing two openings per ad, or Monthly Content for six videos delivered in two batches.

Can I use the videos in paid ads?

All packages include organic use on your brand’s channels. Ad Creative Pack includes 30 days of paid use from your brand’s account. Request a quote for extended paid use or other packages, and tell me which platforms you plan to use so we can account for their AI disclosure rules.

Can I review the direction and request changes?

Yes. You approve the creative direction before production and review the first draft. Signature Reel includes one edit revision, and Monthly Content includes one revision per video. I’ll confirm the revision scope for your package in the quote.

How soon can I see a first draft?

Allow 3–5 business days for one video, 5–7 for three, and 7–10 for six, after payment, receipt of your assets and creative approval. Share your launch date upfront so we can agree on the schedule.

Can I request a different format or a custom package?

Yes. Tell me which placements you need and how many looks, edits or openings you want. I’ll confirm formats and pricing before production.
