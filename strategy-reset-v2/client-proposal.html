2 · Why month one didn't break through

An honest read — so the fix is targeted, not guesswork:

  • The hook lost the first 3 seconds. Static opens get scrolled past before the message lands.
  • We led with polish, not friction. The feed rewards a sharp, contrarian opening line, not a calm intro.
  • No proven structure underneath the posts. We wrote good content, but not content shaped like what already wins in this niche.
  • No paid reach. Organic-only on cold accounts has almost no distribution. There was no lever filling the top of the funnel.

3 · The new direction — model what already wins, then put spend behind it

Step 1 — Reverse-engineer proven winners

We study posts in the business-acquisition / "boring wealth" niche that have already done millions of views. We break down the structure that made them work — hook, pacing, on-screen text, the emotional turn, the call to action.

We model the proven structure, then write original Anya content into it. The framework is borrowed; the script, voice and brand stay 100% ours.

Step 2 — Build for all three platforms

Each idea is produced as native content for YouTube, Instagram and TikTok — correct format, length and end-card per platform — using the AI avatar + motion stack.

One proven structure → three platform-native assets. More surface area, same production effort.

Step 3 — Paid boost: the reach lever (7-day push)

Once a post is live and performing, we put paid spend behind the best ones for a full week across all three platforms. This is what gets Anya in front of large, cold audiences fast — the difference between a good post nobody sees and a good post that compounds followers and signups. Organic earns the right; paid pours fuel on it.

5 · Restructured performance bonus — tied to what we control

Whether a warm lead converts to a paid challenge depends on the sales page, the price and the follow-up — none of which we run. So we're proposing to re-tie the same reward to the metric we do own and drive directly:

6 · The 10-day test & decision

We run the new engine on 10 scripts over 10 days, with the 7-day paid push behind the best performers. At the end we read the numbers together — reach, follower growth, and free-community signups — and decide whether to scale, adjust, or stop. No open-ended commitment; a clear checkpoint.
