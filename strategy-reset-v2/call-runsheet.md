## 1 · Close out Milestone 1 — $750 *(do this first)*
- The first 15 days are **complete, contracted work** — independent of whatever we decide next.
- Ask plainly: **"Can we release the $750 for the first block today so we start the next cycle clean?"**
- If they hesitate on results → "That milestone is for the production and delivery, which is done. The reach problem is exactly what the new plan solves — and I'm not asking you to fund that blindly, the whole budget is laid out."

## 2 · Why it underperformed (don't dwell — 60 seconds)
Hit the four reasons, fast and matter-of-fact:
1. Hook lost the first 3 seconds (static opens).
2. Led with polish, not a contrarian opening line.
3. No proven structure under the posts.
4. **No paid reach** — organic-only on cold accounts barely distributes.

> "None of these are fatal. They're all fixable, and the fix is the new direction."

## 3 · The new direction (the core pitch)
- **Model what already wins:** find posts in his niche doing *millions of views*, break down the *structure* — hook, pacing, on-screen text, the turn, the CTA. Write original Anya content into that proven shape.
  - If he worries about copying: *"We borrow the structure, not the content. Script, voice and brand stay 100% ours. This is how every top creator works — they don't reinvent the format, they reuse what the algorithm already rewards."*
- **Three platforms native:** one structure → YouTube + Instagram + TikTok assets.
- **Paid boost, 7-day push:** put spend behind the winners. *"This is the lever that was missing. It's the difference between a good post nobody sees and one that compounds followers and signups."*

## 4 · The budget (be transparent, lead with the total)
> "Here's everything it costs to run one cycle — no surprises."

- **Tool stack:** $235/mo — HeyGen, Higgsfield, Claude, Metricool, ElevenLabs, CapCut, Canva.
- **Paid boost:** $595 for a 7-day push across all 3 (recommend Standard; can go Lean ~$315 or Aggressive ~$1,050).
- **+10% buffer** → **one-cycle total ≈ $915.**
- Note: anything he already pays for, we deduct.

## 5 · Restructure the bonus (your key ask)
Frame it as *better for him*, because it is:
> "The old bonus was $500 for the first 40 *paid* signups. Problem: whether a lead converts to paid depends on the sales page, price and follow-up — things I don't control. So I'd be paid on something outside my hands, which isn't fair to either of us."

> "What I *do* control is getting people into the **free community**. So I want to tie the same $500 to that — **$5 per verified free-community member, target 100**. Same budget, but now it's tied to a number I can actually move — and it builds you an owned audience you keep marketing to for free."

- **Scalable hook:** "If you want more momentum, we just raise the target at the same rate — 250 members for $1,000. You decide the dial."
- If he pushes on rate: $5/signup is the anchor. You can flex the *target up*, not the rate down.

## 6 · The test & decision (lowers his risk)
> "We run 10 scripts over 10 days with the paid push behind the best ones, then read the numbers together — reach, followers, free signups — and decide to scale, adjust, or stop. No open-ended commitment."

## 7 · Payment — give him the choice
- **Option A — milestone release:** settles in ~7 days, stays on-platform.
- **Option B — direct to your US account:** clears in ~2 days, faster start.
> "Either works for me. It's just how fast you want the next cycle moving — if you want it live this week, the direct route is quicker."

## Likely objections → your answer
- **"Why pay $750 if it didn't go viral?"** → It's for delivered production, not a virality guarantee. Reach is what the *new* plan fixes — fully budgeted, your call to fund.
- **"Isn't copying viral posts risky/unoriginal?"** → We model structure, not content. Standard practice for every serious creator. Brand stays ours.
- **"Why should I pay for free signups, not sales?"** → Because that's the part I control and can guarantee effort against. Sales depend on your funnel. You also keep the audience.
- **"$915 is a lot."** → It's mostly *your* ad spend going to *your* reach, plus tools you keep. We can run the Lean tier (~$315 boost) if you'd rather start smaller.
- **"Can we skip paid boost?"** → That's the exact thing that was missing in month one. Without it we'd be repeating what didn't work.
