Questions

Good to know.

Do I need any tech skills?

None. Type what you want (or pick a template), tap generate, download. That's it.

How do I pay?

Paystack secure checkout — card, bank transfer or USSD, in naira. Credits land instantly.

What are credits?

Each generation uses credits. You get 12 free to start, then top up with a pack or a monthly plan. Credits from packs never expire.

Can I create a consistent face/avatar?

Yes — upload one selfie in the Avatar Studio and generate yourself in any scene with the same face.

Can I install it like an app?

Yes — open it on your phone and tap "Add to Home Screen". It runs full-screen like a native app.
