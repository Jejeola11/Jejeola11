The Sample-First Method: make the work before they hire you

Here's the whole idea, and it is almost stupidly simple once you see it.

Everybody else asks for the job. You do the job — a small piece of it, unasked — and send it over. Not a proposal. Not a CV. Not "I'd love to work with you." The actual finished thing, with their real product in it.
