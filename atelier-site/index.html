GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you should know before joining Fuse Atelier — what you get, how it works, and what happens after you start.

What exactly is Fuse Atelier?
Fuse Atelier is a practical Learn → Create → Sell ecosystem. You learn a commercial AI skill inside the Academy, turn it into proof inside Fuse Create, then use Fuse Clients and the Money Engine to research prospects, pitch, follow up and keep opportunities moving.
LearnCreateSell
How does the 30-day money-back guarantee work?
Fuse Atelier includes a 30-Day First-Client Action Guarantee. Complete the required lessons and assignments, follow the prospecting, pitching and follow-up process for the full 30 days, and keep the required action tracker. If you do all of that and still do not land your first paying client, you can submit the completed tracker and request a refund of your ₦15,000 access payment. The guarantee is based on implementation, not passive course watching.
Who is Fuse Atelier for?
It is built for beginners, creatives, freelancers, students and anyone who wants to turn AI-assisted skills into work they can actually show and sell. You do not need to already be a designer, video creator or experienced freelancer.
Can I join as a complete beginner with no prior AI experience?
Yes. The learning paths start from the workflow itself and move toward practical output. The goal is not to overwhelm you with every AI tool — it is to help you understand a useful process, practise it, and produce something you can use as proof of skill.
Is this just a course library, or do I actually get tools?
It is more than a video library. Founding access includes the Academy, Fuse Create, the Fuse Clients pipeline, starter AI credits, prompts, templates, resources and the private community. The pieces are designed to connect so you can move from lesson to sample to prospect without treating each step like a separate system.
What skills can I learn inside Fuse Atelier?
The current core paths are Design & Flyers, AI UGC & Influencer, Landing Pages, and The Money Engine. The first three focus on creating sellable work; the Money Engine focuses on finding prospects, pitching, following up and turning that work into client opportunities.
Can I use Fuse Atelier on my phone?
Yes. Fuse Atelier is being built mobile-first, so the core experience is designed to work from your phone. Some third-party creative tools may still feel easier on a laptop depending on the tool and the type of project you are creating.
How much time do I need each week?
The Academy is self-paced. You can learn around school, work or other responsibilities, but implementation matters more than simply watching lessons. A few focused hours used to learn, create a sample and do outreach will take you further than collecting tutorials without practising.
AI changes fast. Won't the training become outdated?
Fuse Atelier is designed around workflows, not dependence on one permanent tool. The platform, lessons and resources can keep evolving as better methods appear. Third-party AI products can change, disappear or alter their pricing, so the focus is learning how to adapt the workflow instead of memorising one interface forever.
What happens when my starter AI credits finish?
Your Fuse Atelier access does not disappear. AI generation itself uses credits, so when your included starter credits are exhausted, additional generation can be topped up when you need it rather than pretending one founding payment can fund unlimited AI usage forever.
Will Fuse Atelier help me find clients?
Yes — client acquisition is part of the system. The Money Engine and Fuse Clients help you research prospects, identify visible problems, create relevant proof, pitch, follow up and track the pipeline. It is not an income guarantee: results still depend on your skill, prospect selection, outreach volume, consistency and follow-up.
How is this different from free YouTube tutorials?
Free tutorials can teach isolated tricks. Fuse Atelier is built around the entire commercial loop: learn a skill, build proof, find a business that needs it, package the sample, pitch it and follow up. The value is the connected system, practical resources and implementation path — not simply having more videos to watch.
Is the ₦15,000 founding price monthly?
No. The current ₦15,000 Founding Access price is one payment for the Fuse Atelier access described on this page. AI generation remains credit-based because generation has a real ongoing usage cost.
What happens after I click “Get Founding Access”?
The checkout button opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. You will receive the current payment instructions there, and your access is set up after payment is confirmed.
