Before you choose

Questions you are probably already asking.

I am a complete beginner. Which plan should I choose?

Core is the starting point if you can follow the system independently. Fast Track is for you if you want help actually completing one skill, building your proof and packaging the offer.

Do I need a laptop?

Fuse Atelier is designed around a mobile-first workflow. Some tools can be easier on a larger screen, but the core system is built so you can start from your phone.

What exactly is the Money Engine?

It is the client-acquisition layer behind the skills: how to identify prospects, build samples, price your service, pitch, follow up and keep your outreach organized.

What are the Fuse Create credits for?

Credits let you use supported creation tools inside Fuse Create. Core includes a small starter pack; Fast Track includes enough credits for the agreed one-skill implementation path; VIP receives an execution allocation for the agreed plan.

Does VIP guarantee I will get a client in 30 days?

No. VIP gives you direct 1:1 execution support for 30 days while we work toward your first client together. Client outcomes still depend on your execution, market response and the offers you take to market.

Can I pay Fast Track or VIP in installments?

Yes. Fast Track can be presented as ₦119,000 once or 2 × ₦65,000. VIP can be presented as ₦499,000 once or 2 × ₦275,000.
