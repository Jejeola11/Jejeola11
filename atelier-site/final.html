Before you choose

Questions you are probably already asking.

What exactly do I get when I join?

You get access to Fuse Academy’s three AI courses, Fuse Create, your personal AI Client Agent, the Money Engine, the Affiliate Programme, starter credits, templates, resources, community and the current implementation support included in your plan.

Do I get all three AI courses?

Yes. Fuse Core gives you access to all three current courses: Design & Flyers, AI UGC & Influencer, and Landing Page Design. You can start with one and return to the others when you are ready.

What is the Client Agent?

It is your personal research assistant inside Fuse. You tell it what you sell, who you want to work with and where you want to target. It helps you find qualified businesses, understand their opportunity and prepare a relevant pitch. It does not guarantee a client or income.

Are Create generations and Client Agent searches unlimited?

No. Access to the tools is included, but generations and research use credits. Fuse Core includes starter credits so you can begin creating proof. Additional credits can be topped up when you need more.

I am a complete beginner. Which plan should I choose?

Fuse Core is the starting point. It gives you the full learning, proof-building and client-acquisition system, plus group implementation support.

Do I need a laptop?

Fuse Atelier is designed around a mobile-first workflow. Some tools can be easier on a larger screen, but the core system is built so you can start from your phone.

What exactly is the Money Engine?

It is the client-acquisition layer behind the skills: how to identify prospects, build samples, price your service, pitch, follow up and keep your outreach organized.

What are the Fuse Create credits for?

Credits let you use supported creation tools inside Fuse Create. Fuse Core includes credits for portfolio-ready work and one prospect-specific sample. 1:1 Intensive includes everything in Core.

Does the 1:1 Intensive guarantee I will get a client?

No. The Intensive gives you direct guidance on your skill, offer, proof and first outreach steps. Results still depend on your implementation, market response and the quality of the work you take to market.

Can I pay for the 1:1 Intensive in installments?

Yes. The 1:1 Intensive is ₦89,000 once or 2 × ₦50,000. Message Ria to confirm the current founding spots before making an installment payment.
