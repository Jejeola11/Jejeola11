Fuse Auto — Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 1 July 2026

Fuse Auto stores only the connected Instagram account's access token and the automation settings the account owner created. You can delete this data at any time:

Option 1 — Disconnect in the app

  1. Open the Fuse Auto dashboard.
  2. Reconnect or remove the connection; the stored access token is overwritten/invalidated.

Option 2 — Revoke on Instagram

  1. Open Instagram → your profile → Settings and privacy.
  2. Go to Apps and websites.
  3. Find Fuse Auto and tap Remove. This immediately revokes the app's access token.

Option 3 — Email request

Email riadigitals0@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion". All stored data associated with the account will be permanently deleted within 30 days, and a confirmation will be sent.
