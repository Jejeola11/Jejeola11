Fuse Auto — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 July 2026

Fuse Auto ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal Instagram automation tool operated by the owner of the connected Instagram account (@director_ria / @fuse_studio2). This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

What the app does

When someone comments a chosen keyword on one of the account owner's Instagram posts, Fuse Auto automatically posts a public reply to that comment and sends the commenter a private direct message with a relevant link. That is the app's only function.

Information we process

  • Instagram account credentials — an access token for the single connected Instagram account, obtained through Instagram's official login. It is stored securely on our hosting provider (Netlify) and used only to reply to comments and send messages from that account.
  • Comment data — when a comment triggers an automation, we process the comment's ID and text to decide whether it matches a keyword and to send a reply. We do not build profiles of commenters.
  • Automation settings — the keywords, reply text, and message text configured by the account owner.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not use the data for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not access private messages, followers lists, or any content beyond the comments that trigger an automation on the owner's own posts.
  • We do not act on behalf of any account other than the single connected one.

Data storage & retention

Data is stored on Netlify's infrastructure. The access token and automation settings are retained until the account owner disconnects the app or deletes them. Transient comment/message data is not permanently stored.

Data deletion

The account owner can remove all stored data at any time by disconnecting the app (which invalidates the stored token) or by revoking access in Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites. To request deletion of any data, contact us at the email below and it will be removed within 30 days. See also our Data Deletion instructions.
