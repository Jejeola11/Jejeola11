⚙️ First-time setup (tap to open — you only do this once)

Before the button above can connect, your Meta app needs to exist. ~5 minutes, once.

  1. Go to developers.facebook.com/apps → Create app → Business → add the Instagram product. Your IG must be a Business/Creator account.
  2. On the Instagram product page, copy your App ID and App secret.
  3. In this Netlify site → Site settings → Environment variables, add these 4, then redeploy:
    APP_ID = your app id · APP_SECRET = your app secret · VERIFY_TOKEN = any word (e.g. fuse123)

    NETLIFY_BLOBS_TOKEN = a Netlify Personal Access Token. Get one at app.netlify.com → your avatar → User settings → Applications → New access token.

On developers.facebook.com → your app → App roles → Roles → Add Instagram Testers → type your IG username → Submit. Then on Instagram (web or app) → Settings → Apps and websites → accept the invite.
