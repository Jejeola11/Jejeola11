⚙️ First-time setup (tap to open — you only do this once)

Before the button above can connect, your Meta app needs to exist. ~5 minutes, once.

  1. Go to developers.facebook.com/apps → Create app → Business → add the Instagram product. Your IG must be a Business/Creator account.
  2. On the Instagram product page, copy your App ID and App secret.
  3. In this Netlify site → Site settings → Environment variables, add these 3, then redeploy:
    APP_ID = your app id · APP_SECRET = your app secret · VERIFY_TOKEN = any word (e.g. fuse123)
  4. In the Meta app → Instagram → set up Instagram login → under Valid OAuth Redirect URIs, paste this and save:
  5. In the Meta app → Instagram → Webhooks → Add callback URL below + your VERIFY_TOKEN, then subscribe to both the comments AND messaging_postbacks fields (the second one is needed for "Send me the LINK" buttons to work):
  6. One Instagram account needs a role on the app before it can connect (you'll see "insufficient developer role" otherwise). On developers.facebook.com/apps → your app → left menu App roles → Roles → Add Instagram Testers → type your IG username → Submit. Then open the Instagram app on your phone → your profile → Settings → Apps and websites → Tester invites → Accept. (Nothing will show there until you've sent the invite from this step first.)

Done with these? Tap Connect Instagram above — no token copying needed. 🎉
