Frequently asked questions

How do credits work?

Each Fuse Create model or tool has its own credit cost. Your balance is deducted only when you run a supported generation.

Is this a subscription?

No. These are one-time credit top-ups. You can buy another pack whenever you want more credits.

Why does Power say 23% off per credit?

Starter works out to ₦100 per credit. Power works out to about ₦76.92 per credit, which is roughly 23% lower.

What does the dollar price mean?

The USD amount is shown as a simple international price reference. The available checkout currency depends on the payment method connected to your account.

Prices shown are for one-time credit top-ups. Different AI models use different numbers of credits per generation.
