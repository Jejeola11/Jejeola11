Before you top up

What can I make with a small pack?

Small packs let you try lower-cost tools. Premium videos and longer generations may need more credits than a trial pack contains. Check the cost of your chosen tool first.

How do I pay and receive credits?

Choose your pack and pay through Nestuge. Use the WhatsApp link in your Nestuge product item or welcome email to send your Fuse account email and payment reference. Ria will verify the successful payment and manually add your credits. This is not instant delivery.

Does buying credits unlock courses or the AI agent?

No. Academy courses are separate purchases. The AI agent requires access to the Money Engine course or the 14 Days Challenge; credit purchases do not unlock it.

What happens to my existing credits?

Your existing credit balance is retained. The credit cost of a new generation can change as model prices and settings change.

Does every pack give the same value?

The ₦1,000 pack includes 4 credits and the ₦5,000 pack includes 45. Packs from ₦10,000 include one credit for every ₦100.
