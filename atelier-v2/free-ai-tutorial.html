Create In Fuse ↗BEFORE YOU START

Three things to set

Read this once,
then you are set

01

Set your name first

The video prompt keeps the placeholder [NAME] in the spoken lines — 4 times, highlighted throughout. Swap it for the name the photographer should call out.

02

Want a different outfit?

Rewrite the OUTFIT section of the image prompt, or drop in a reference photo of the piece as @image2 and put same outfit from @image2 in that section instead. Everything else stays untouched.

03

About the sound

The prompt asks for natural audio only — wind, water, footsteps, laughter, dialogue. No music. If you want a camera shutter under the shots or a track over the whole clip, add it afterwards in your video editing app. That way the generated audio stays clean underneath.

Create In Fuse ↗STEP 01 · IMAGE

Nano Banana Pro · 9:16

The image prompt

Your selfie goes in as @image1. What comes out is the still you will animate on the next page.

MODEL
Nano Banana Pro
RATIO
9:16
INPUT
1 selfie

Your selfie as @image1
Nothing else.

THE IMAGE PROMPT
Use @image1 as the identity and appearance reference for the person only. Preserve the recognizable identity, facial features, hairstyle, body proportions, and overall appearance of the person from @image1. Do not redesign or replace the person.
Create a highly realistic luxury lifestyle fashion photograph in a photographer POV composition.
The scene takes place outdoors in an elegant modern backyard at golden hour, beside a large stylish swimming pool and a sophisticated outdoor kitchen area. The location should closely resemble a refined luxury villa setting, with a similar atmosphere, structure, and visual feel: modern architecture, calm pool water, soft warm light, elegant outdoor materials, and a premium summer ambiance.
PERSON
Person from @image1 stands a few steps in front of the photographer near the outdoor kitchen counter and very close to the pool.
The person is slightly turned away from the camera, with the back and side of the body facing the photographer, while naturally turning the head back over the shoulder and looking directly toward the photographer.
The expression should feel soft, confident, feminine, elegant, and relaxed.
The pose should feel natural and candid, as if the photographer captured the person during a beautiful summer evening by the pool.
The person is holding an elegant cocktail glass in one hand.
OUTFIT
Dress the person in a beautiful, elegant, feminine luxury summer outfit suitable for an upscale villa and poolside setting.
The outfit should feel light, airy, refined, and effortlessly chic, with a soft feminine silhouette and lightweight premium fabrics suitable for warm summer weather.
Keep the styling elegant and versatile, but make it more summery and colorful rather than plain neutral. Use a fresh, tasteful Mediterranean-inspired color palette with soft sunlit tones such as peach, apricot, warm coral, champagne, cream, sand, light beige, soft gold, or subtle touches of turquoise or sky blue.
The colors should feel harmonious, sophisticated, and luxurious, never too loud, overly bold, or heavily patterned.
The overall look should feel breezy, fresh, modern, flattering, and high-end, with a relaxed luxury vacation mood.
Use subtle refined jewelry and minimal elegant accessories.
Optional lightly tinted sunglasses may be worn casually or placed nearby.
No handbag in her hand, because she is holding the cocktail glass.
ENVIRONMENT
- elegant modern villa in the background
- large stylish pool with calm blue water
- the pool should feel visually prominent and spacious
- sophisticated outdoor kitchen / grill station beside the person
- visible kitchen counter where the drink could naturally be placed down
- open-air lounge and dining area visible in the background
- refined outdoor furniture
- greenery and palm trees
- warm summer atmosphere
- soft golden evening light
- premium architectural details
- luxury backyard setting
- visually similar feel to an upscale modern Mediterranean-style villa exterior
COMPOSITION
- photographer POV
- both of the photographer’s hands visible in the foreground
- a professional DSLR camera visible in the foreground
- the DSLR rear screen should realistically show the person framed in the shot
- Person from @image1 is the clear subject in the middle ground
- the person is the primary focal point of the image
- vertical composition
- elegant depth
- realistic photographic perspective
- cinematic but realistic
- candid luxury vacation aesthetic
- premium editorial fashion photography feel
IMPORTANT FOCUS
The focus must be locked precisely on Person from @image1.
Person from @image1 must be perfectly sharp, crisp, and highly detailed, especially in the face, hair, skin, and outfit.
Do not make the person blurry, soft, or out of focus.
The face must remain clearly visible and recognizable.
The DSLR camera in the foreground may be slightly softer than the subject if needed, but the person must remain the sharp focal point.
Only the distant background may have a subtle natural blur for depth.
IMPORTANT REALISM
- realistic skin texture
- visible pores
- natural anatomy
- believable hands
- realistic lighting
- realistic shadows
- natural fabric folds and texture
- realistic reflections on the DSLR camera and screen
- no plastic skin
- no over-smoothed AI look
- no unrealistic body proportions
- no fake or distorted hands
- no excessive blur on the person
The final result should feel like a real luxury vacation fashion photograph: Person from @image1 standing beside a large modern villa pool and an outdoor kitchen at golden hour, holding a cocktail glass, wearing an elegant light and summery luxury outfit, and naturally turning back over the shoulder while being photographed from behind the camera.

Seedance 2.5 · 9:16

The video prompt

Two inputs, and the slots swap: the still from step 01 goes in as @image1, your selfie as @image2.

MODEL
Seedance 2.5
RATIO
9:16
INPUTS
@image1 + @image2
LENGTH
25 s

@image1 start frame

@image2 your selfie

THE VIDEO PROMPT Replace [NAME] · 4×
25-SECOND CONTINUOUS VIDEO — SAME CHARACTER, SAME LOCATION

REFERENCE

Use @image1 as exact opening frame, location, outfit and lighting.

Use @image2 as facial identity lock.

Keep the same facial identity from @image2 and the same hairstyle, body, outfit, accessories and styling from @image1 throughout.

No identity or outfit drift.


SETTING

Use the exact villa garden and pool environment from @image1.

Natural summer audio only: wind, birds, water, footsteps, fabric, laughter and dialogue.

No music.


STRUCTURE

0–4s RAW DSLR PHOTOGRAPHER POV

4–11s CINEMATIC HANDHELD FASHION MONTAGE, 0.75x–0.80x SLOW MOTION

11–25s RAW PHOTOGRAPHER POV — ONE UNBROKEN TAKE TO THE END


0–4s — RAW DSLR POV

Begin exactly like @image1.

DSLR body, lens, rear LCD, both photographer hands and wrist stay visible at the bottom the entire time.

Natural BTS shake, breathing movement, small reframing, slight autofocus/exposure shifts.

Photographer:

“[NAME], turn a little more… perfect.”

The person from @image1 smiles and places the cocktail upright on the bar.

Photographer:

“Now walk toward the pool.”

The person walks toward the pool with a confident editorial runway walk: clear pronounced hip sway side to side, one foot slightly crossing in front of the other, relaxed shoulders, natural outfit movement.

The person looks back over one shoulder once while the photographer follows.

DSLR remains visible.


4–11s — CINEMATIC HANDHELD FASHION MONTAGE

At 4s DSLR disappears.

Warm golden-hour fashion-film look based on the lighting and color palette of @image1, shallow depth of field, soft glow, creamy bokeh, organic handheld drift and slow motion.

Every shot MUST use a DIFFERENT POSE, ANGLE and camera distance.

No repeated walking shot, no slideshow.


4–5.5s LOW ANGLE:

Camera near the pool beside the person’s feet.

The person takes two elegant steps with visible hip sway; outfit moves naturally.

Camera tilts upward slightly.


5.5–7s EXTREME DEPTH OF FIELD / RACK FOCUS:

Tight three-quarter side close-up.

Blurred environmental element from @image1 in foreground.

The person holds a new pose, one hand softly at the waist, shoulders angled away, face turned to lens.

Focus starts on foreground and racks smoothly to the eyes; background melts into creamy bokeh.


7–8.5s SIDE PROFILE:

The person shifts weight onto one leg, lifts chin and runs one hand naturally through or near the hair.

Camera slides gently sideways, still handheld.

Outfit moves naturally in the breeze.


8.5–10s HIGH DIAGONAL ANGLE:

Camera looks slightly down toward the person beside the pool.

New pose: one leg forward, one hand near the neck, the other relaxed by the thigh.

The person looks up and slowly turns the shoulders.

Pool reflections move behind.


10–11s HERO CLOSE-UP:

Frontal handheld medium close-up.

Intense editorial gaze becomes a genuine smile.

Camera drifts closer, briefly loses focus, finds the eyes again.

The person glances mischievously toward the pool.


11–14s — RAW POV SETUP

Return to normal speed and raw early-2000s handheld energy: stronger shake, imperfect framing, washed highlights, low contrast, soft detail, autofocus hunting, zero stabilization.

IMPORTANT:

DSLR body, rear LCD, BOTH HANDS and wrist are visible again exactly like the opening.

From 11s until the final underwater frame there is NO CUT, NO EXTERNAL CAMERA and NO ANGLE CHANGE — one continuous first-person photographer POV.

The person laughs and steps backward toward the pool edge while facing the photographer.

Photographer:

“[NAME]… what are you doing?”

The person keeps eye contact, opens both arms and says:

“Just keep filming.”


14–17s — BACKWARD FALL

NO CUT.

DSLR and both hands stay clearly visible in the lower foreground.

The person remains facing the photographer with the pool directly behind.

The person opens both arms fully and performs a slow elegant backward trust fall.

The feet stay grounded at first while the entire body leans backward as one long connected line.

The body passes the balance point, gravity takes over, and the person falls BACK-FIRST into the pool in one smooth motion.

No jump, no dive, no backflip, no turning around.

Outfit and hair move naturally.

Photographer reacts slightly late and tilts down to follow.

Photographer:

“Oh my God— [NAME]!”

The person hits the water with one large natural splash.


17–20s — CONTINUOUS REACTION

Same unbroken POV.

Photographer immediately runs to the pool while still holding the DSLR in both hands.

Heavy footstep shake, brief accidental dip toward the terrace, then reframes.

DSLR NEVER disappears.

The person does not immediately resurface.

The person stays underwater and clearly swims directly toward the photographer.

Outfit and hair move naturally underwater.

Photographer, laughing:

“You’re actually crazy!”

The person reaches the edge, rises to the surface soaked and laughing, looks directly toward the photographer and reaches one wet hand upward.

[NAME]:

“Come on!”

Water splashes toward the lens.


20–25s — CONTINUOUS DSLR DISCARD / JUMP / UNDERWATER ENDING

NO CUT.

Photographer laughs:

“No way—”

He suddenly decides to jump in.

IMPORTANT:

He does NOT enter the pool holding the DSLR.

He quickly turns toward a nearby dry surface beside the pool and visibly tosses or places the DSLR safely aside.

The DSLR physically leaves his hands and stays outside the water.

The same first-person POV continues without a cut.

He immediately turns back and jumps into the pool.

Same POV lunges forward and down and crashes through the water surface.

Stay underwater for several seconds:

bubbles rush past the lens,
light ripples above,
outfit moves naturally,
hair floats freely,
sound becomes muffled.

The person swims directly toward the camera smiling, reaches playfully toward the lens and gets close.

End on the person near the camera through bubbles and shimmering light.


NEGATIVE

No face or outfit change, identity drift, duplicate limbs, warped hands, extra fingers, random objects, static poses, repeated cinematic angles, slideshow, gimbal look, broken continuity or any cut from 11–25s.

No forward dive, no sideways fall, no turning around before falling, no backflip, no jumping upward before the fall, no disappearing DSLR before it is visibly placed aside, no DSLR underwater.
