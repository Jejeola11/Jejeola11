Scene: a refined contemporary villa beside an outdoor swimming pool at late golden hour. Warm sunlight touches the terrace, calm water reflects the sky, and palm trees and an outdoor kitchen sit in the background. She stands near the pool, turning her head back toward the photographer with a relaxed welcoming smile. Keep a safe, natural distance from the pool edge.

Camera: the viewer is the photographer. A realistic camera and both photographer hands appear low in the foreground, with the camera's rear screen accurately showing the same subject and pose. Make the woman the sharp focal point; keep the camera foreground slightly softer and the distant villa gently out of focus. Editorial composition, natural skin texture, believable fingers, coherent shadows, premium but realistic color. No extra people, logos, text, warped camera screen, altered identity, redesigned outfit or duplicate limbs.

Middle: move into a few elegant editorial portraits around the same terrace. Alternate a medium shot, a closer face shot and a wider view that shows the dress moving naturally. Keep the sun direction, wardrobe details and facial features consistent. Give each shot a distinct pose and camera distance; avoid a slideshow effect.

Ending: return to the photographer's point of view as the subject moves playfully toward the water. If a pool-entry moment is generated, show a physically coherent action and water reaction, then finish with the subject visible in or near the water. Keep the sequence modest, graceful and believable. Photographer may call, '[NAME], one more look,' once. Use natural ambient sound and clear dialogue only; no background music.

Preserve continuity across the whole clip. No face swaps, outfit changes, floating accessories, duplicate hands, distorted camera display, sudden location changes, impossible motion, extra subjects, text or logos.
