Think of it this way

“I design” tells people your skill. “I create launch graphics for skincare brands” tells a business exactly how you can help.

Do this now

  1. Write down the skill you can already use.
  2. Choose one type of business you understand or want to help.
  3. Choose one small result your work can help them get.
