GOT ANY QUESTIONS LEFT?

Who can join?

Anyone with a Fuse account can share their referral link. Your personal link appears in the workspace below.

How much do I earn?

One credit for each eligible new signup, 5% of verified credit-pack payments and 50% of verified Academy course payments from referred users.

Is commission recurring?

Each eligible purchase may earn its stated commission. The calculator does not assume repeat purchases or a guaranteed monthly income.

What does my referral get?

A new referral may request 25% off their first credit pack within 24 hours of opening their account. The discounted purchase is handled through the claim flow while our Nestuge checkout remains manually verified.

How are cash rewards paid?

We record eligible commissions after payment verification and review them before payout. Your recorded amounts appear in your workspace.

Can I use my own link?

No. Self-referrals and accounts that already existed do not earn signup rewards.
