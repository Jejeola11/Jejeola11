GOT ANY QUESTIONS LEFT?

Who can join?

Anyone with a Fuse account can share their referral link. Tap Start Earning Today to copy your personal link.

How much do I earn?

One credit for each eligible new signup, 5% of verified credit-pack payments and 50% of verified Academy course payments from referred users.

How long does a referral keep earning me commission?

For six months from the day they become your referral. Earn 5% on each successful credit-pack purchase and 50% on each successful Academy purchase during that period. Repeat purchases qualify too.

What does my referral get?

New referrals get 25% off their first credit pack when purchased within 24 hours of creating their account. Eligible discounts are applied automatically at checkout.

How are commissions tracked and paid?

Successful Paystack payments automatically record eligible commissions in your dashboard. Add your bank details there for monthly payouts. Payment verification and credit or course delivery are automatic.

Can I use my own link?

No. Self-referrals and accounts that already existed do not earn signup rewards.
