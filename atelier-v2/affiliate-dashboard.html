FUSE CREATOR VIDEO IDEAS

Make it yours. Make it useful.

These are starting points, not scripts. Use your voice, your niche, and your own creative direction. Your video must show Fuse honestly and include your affiliate disclosure.

Quick formatHook in the first seconds → show the result or process → say who Fuse helps → add your link CTA.
01 · Testimonial / discovery

“I just found an AI platform that actually helps creatives get clients.”

Show yourself discovering Fuse, then cut to the course, creation tools, and Client section. Keep it like you are telling a friend.

02 · Build with me

“Come with me while I create a client-ready visual on my phone.”

Start with the final result first. Then show a fast, honest screen recording of how Fuse helped you make it.

03 · Skill-to-income angle

“If I had to start freelancing today, this is the route I’d take.”

Explain the Fuse flow: learn one skill, create a sample, find a business, then pitch them.

04 · Three things I built

“Three things I made with Fuse Atelier this week.”

Use a quick before/after or gallery format: flyer, UGC concept, landing page, or client sample.

05 · Beginner reality check

“You do not need to know everything about AI before you start.”

Speak to beginners feeling stuck. Show the exact one skill or tool you would begin with inside Fuse.

06 · Screen-recorded tutorial

“Watch me turn this idea into a proper client sample.”

Teach one small, real step with a screen recording and voice-over. Give value first; mention Fuse naturally at the end.

07 · POV / relatable story

“POV: you’re tired of learning skills but still don’t know how to get a client.”

Use a relatable opening, then show how the Client and Academy sections connect the missing steps.

08 · Honest comparison

“What I wish I had when I started learning creative AI.”

Talk about the gap between random tutorials and a practical path. Do not make income promises or claim results you cannot verify.

Keep it strong
  • Film vertically and make the first moment clear.
  • Use your own voice, footage, or AI creation — no stolen content.
  • Show real Fuse screens or work you actually made.
  • Write: “Affiliate link — I may earn a commission if you join.”
