BEFORE YOU JOIN

A few things to know.

Do I need previous clients or a big audience?

No. You do need an existing AI video skill and examples of work you’ve made. We’ll help you organise relevant proof and approach businesses directly.

What if I have already pitched and nobody replied?

Bring three previous messages and any replies to onboarding. We’ll look at your offer, portfolio, prospect fit and messages to identify what needs improving.

Are the lessons live?

The 14 daily video guides are prerecorded on the platform, with action steps. Ria provides daily group task check-ins and guidance during the challenge, plus three scheduled individual reviews. Confirm the evening check-in time and replay-access period with Ria before payment.

Will I definitely get a foreign client in 14 days?

No. The goal is paid international work, but client decisions and results vary. If you complete the daily tasks and send 50 relevant personalised pitches without securing a paid client, the offer includes 14 additional days of guided support.

Will you give me clients or send the pitches for me?

You will research and approach businesses yourself using the lessons, resources and feedback. This is a guided challenge, not a placement service or done-for-you outreach service.

What does the instalment option cover?

The total fee is ₦15,000. You can pay ₦7,500 first and the remaining ₦7,500 by October 18, before the October 19 start. Ask Ria to confirm your payment and enrolment status.

How do enrolment, access and cancellations work?

Enrolment is currently handled through WhatsApp. Ria will confirm payment instructions, platform access, the support schedule, access duration and cancellation/refund terms before you pay. The extra-support promise is not a money-back guarantee.
