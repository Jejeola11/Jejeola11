# Fuse Rush

Original three-lane endless runner at /atelier-v2/games/fuse-rush.html. All artwork, geometry, sound and mechanics are produced locally by Canvas 2D/Web Audio and the runner engine; no Subway Surfers assets, characters or name are used in the game. No runtime AI calls, credit charges, payment flows or leaderboard server.

Swipe left/right to change lane, up to jump, down to slide; touch buttons and arrow/WASD keys are alternatives. Space jumps; P/Escape pauses. Backgrounding pauses the run. A personal best persists in browser local storage when available. Chrome robot, trains, barriers and low arches are procedural drawings. This is a 2.5D perspective game, not a WebGL production of the commercial game.

Validation: six engine tests cover lane boundaries, pause, coin pickup, collision, jump/slide behavior, navigable obstacle rows and reset. Browser preview confirmed a running canvas, increasing distance/score, collected coins, pause and resume. Physical Android/iOS swipe behavior has not been device-tested.
